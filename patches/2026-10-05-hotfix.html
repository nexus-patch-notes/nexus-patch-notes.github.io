Patch Notes

2026-10-05 Hotfix Patch

2.57.0.98348
Undocumented

General

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where certain Maskless Skins were not appearing in the game.

Heroes

Whitemane

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue causing Desperate Plea's Mana cost to be 5 lower than intended after clearing Desperation stacks.

Xal'atath

Updates

Base

  • Shadow Mark [Q]
    • Void Orb speed slightly increased flight time reduced from 0.7 to 0.65 seconds.
  • Void Step [E]
    • Targeting range reduced from 5 to 2.
    • Distance between teleport points reduced from 7 to 6.5.
    • Now prevented and interrupted by Roots.
  • Void Volley [D]
    • Base damage per missile reduced from 90 to 72.
    • Now deals 50% reduced damage to Structures.
    • Now generates 1 charge for every Basic Attack against enemy Heroes and every 2 Basic Attacks against non-Heroes.
    • Targeting now clamps at maximum range.

Talents

  • Level 1
    • Anchored Core
      • Radius bonus reduced from 50% to 25%.
  • Level 4
    • Dark Barrier
      • Now only triggers when enemy Heroes are hit.
  • Level 7
    • Silence of the Lamb
      • Silence duration increased from 1 to 1.5 seconds.
  • Level 10
    • Void Eruption
      • Uncharged damage reduced by 20%, from 112 to 90.
      • Fully charged damage reduced by 5%, from 175 to 166.
  • Level 13
    • Dark Heart's Protection
      • Detonating the Dark Heart on a target affected by Shadow Mark now Silences all enemies hit, rather than Silencing on cast.
  • Level 20
    • Harbringer's Corruption
      • First charge tier explosion damage reduced by 20%, from 400 to 320.
      • Second charge tier explosion damage reduced by 15%, from 500 to 425.
      • Third charge tier explosion damage reduced by 10%, from 600 to 540.
      • Fourth charge tier explosion damage reduced by 5%, from 700 to 665.
    • Rift Invasion
      • Portals now have non-lethal Health decay, stopping at 4 Health.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where Xal'atath was appearing brighter than intended in the game.
  • Fixed an issue that caused Void Volley impact visual effects to appear at the cursor location instead of where missiles hit.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented the Void Volley charge indicator from immediately displaying 1 charge upon reaching the second Milestone.
  • Fixed an issue causing Anchored Core to bounce on enemy Heroes not in vision, in bushes, or Stealthed.
  • Void Eruption can now be queued to cast or release during Time Stop.
  • Casting Void Eruption during Time Stop immediately enters targeting, with charging beginning when Time Stop ends.
  • Releasing Void Eruption during Time Stop queues the release for when Time Stop ends. The Ability button becomes greyed out while a release is queued.
  • Issuing another Ability, movement, or attack order during Time Stop cancels the pending Void Eruption cast or queued release.
  • Casting Void Eruption again during Time Stop clears earlier queued orders.
  • Fixed issues that could allow an additional Void Eruption charge to begin while already charging or during Void Step.
  • Void Eruption orb growth and the flight damage timer now pause while Xal'atath is affected by Time Stop.
  • Void Eruption orbiting orbs now pause during Time Stop and resume afterward.
  • Fixed issues that prevented Void Eruption orbiting orbs from correctly following the missile or continuing to orbit after a release queued during Time Stop.
  • Fixed an issue causing Void Portals summoned with Void Convergence to be valid targets for allied effects.
